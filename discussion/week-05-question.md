The assignment showed that Lasso outperforms KNN when covariates are independent, while KNN outperforms Lasso when covariates are strongly correlated. Where does Ridge regression fit into this comparison? How might Ridge's test MSE compare with Lasso's and KNN's under independent versus strongly correlated covariates, and why?
